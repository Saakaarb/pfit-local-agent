VISUAL REVIEW OF COMPLETED FULL FITS

18 completed cases; 38 experiment plots inspected. Includes Oregonator's retained DE result after invalid-gradient termination. Running fits untouched.

Output-column mappings audited against saved writeout code. Times and measured columns verified. Lines join saved observation times; no dense reintegration. NRMSE=100*RMSE/max(abs(measured channel)); descriptive, not an acceptance test. Residual=prediction-measurement. Plots alone cannot distinguish model inadequacy from optimization failure.

ARC_fitting: Substantial improvement; remaining systematic mismatch
Thermal-runaway timing and broad shape captured. Temperature is systematically high over the earlier interval (overall RMSE 20.68 in input temperature units). Heating rate has a large initial mismatch and some late-peak error; log RMSE about 0.451 decades. The reported objective mixes normalized temperature/log-heating-rate errors and endpoint penalties; its 94.8% reduction is not a claim of uniformly small trajectory error.
Adam loss reduction: 94.8221%.

armistead_sphingolipid: Mixed; sparse observations
All eight experiments inspected. Sphinga decline captured well. Cer and Sphingo errors vary across replicates, notably experiment 1 Sphingo and experiment 6 Cer. Only two time points per experiment: connecting lines do not validate intervening dynamics, and R-squared is unstable for nearly constant channels.
Adam loss reduction: 2.76289%.

beer_indigoidine: Good overall
Product concentration matches closely. Biomass rises too early and misses the small late decline. Residuals are structured.
Adam loss reduction: 0.000645612%.

boehm_stat5: Good overall
Broad rise and decay captured. Early pSTAT5B peak underestimated; initial/peak mismatch in rSTAT5A and late overprediction in pSTAT5A.
Adam loss reduction: 2.90107e-07%.

cascaded_tanks: Reasonable
Tracks broad changes but misses sharp transitions and the late high plateau. Structured residuals.
Adam loss reduction: 0.010569%.

mapk_cascade: Excellent agreement
Activation trajectory follows measured values closely; small residuals fluctuate around zero. Normalized RMSE about 0.84%.
Adam loss reduction: 3.7379%.

raia_il13: Reasonable overall; peak mismatch
Both stimulation conditions capture the rise and broad plateau/decline. Several central peak observations exceed predictions, particularly at the higher dose; late residuals also remain.
Adam loss reduction: 0.434564%.

schwen_insulin: Poor dynamic agreement
All four predicted insulin signals are nearly flat. They capture approximate signal levels but miss the time variation and peaks in measured signals. Completion and the scalar loss alone therefore overstate the quality of the dynamic fit.
Adam loss reduction: 0.0968824%.

sneyd_ipr: Mixed across nine experiments
All nine plots inspected. Rise-and-decay behavior broadly captured, but peaks are underestimated in experiments 3, 8 and 9; experiments 1 and 2 have excessive post-peak tails. Experiment 7 decays too quickly later. Several other conditions are closer, but systematic errors remain.
Adam loss reduction: 0.0923684%.

oregonator: Poor; refinement degraded
Misses sharp X spikes and underestimates later Z peaks, with phase/baseline errors. Adam stopped on invalid loss or gradient; DE parameters retained. Stored objective is sigma-weighted, unlike the max-data-normalized channel metrics.
Adam loss reduction: 0%.

sliding_basepoint_headered: Good displacement; moderate force mismatch
Displacement shape matches with small negative bias. First force peak underestimated, with shoulder and late-tail errors.
Adam loss reduction: 0%.

decay_multiexp: Excellent
Both experiments overlap the data; residuals about 1e-7 to 1e-6.
Adam loss reduction: 84.4704%.

lotka_volterra: Poor
One broad slow excursion misses the repeated observed peaks. NRMSE about 26.5% and 30.8%; predator R-squared negative.
Adam loss reduction: 2.89175e-11%.

piezo_bouc_wen: Needs improvement
Periodic response present but initial displacement, phase and waveform differ. Peaks lead data; periodic residuals. NRMSE about 13.8%.
Adam loss reduction: 0.000951736%.

robertson_session: Excellent
All states match across the logarithmic time range; channel NRMSE below 0.1%.
Adam loss reduction: 2.94957e-08%.

test_session: Excellent; duplicate Robertson
Same fit as robertson_session; not independent evidence.
Adam loss reduction: 2.94957e-08%.

theophylline: Reasonable to good
Captures the overall rise and decline, but misses early peak timing and underpredicts the late tail. NRMSE about 8.1%. Sparse observation times limit peak-shape assessment.
Adam loss reduction: 4.70613e-07%.

vanderpol_session: Excellent
Oscillation timing and amplitude match. Small residual spikes at fast transitions; channel NRMSE below 0.4%.
Adam loss reduction: 2.4427e-10%.

Auditing user_model.py shows the saved loss compares raw Z (solution[:,0]) with data rather than the declared transformed Ca observable. Thus reported loss0.15165 does not assess the plotted Ca prediction. This is a model/loss inconsistency, not a plotting-column error. No equations or loss were altered during plotting.
