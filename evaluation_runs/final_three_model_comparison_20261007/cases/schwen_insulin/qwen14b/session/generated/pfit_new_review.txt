The initial conditions for each experiment are provided in the frozen dataset. For insulin_10_r1.csv and insulin_10_r2.csv, Ins=10.0, Rec1=58.8503656504712, and Rec2=918.6048612429337. For insulin_100_r1.csv and insulin_100_r2.csv, Ins=100.0, Rec1=58.8503656504712, and Rec2=918.6048612429337. All other states (IR1, IR2, IR1in, IR2in, Uptake1, Uptake2, InsulinFragments, and BoundUnspec) are zero in every record.
The provided information includes all necessary equations and parameters for the ODE system. The RHS expressions for each frozen integrated state are extracted as specified.
The CSV response measurement column 'Insulin_signal' is directly mapped to the simulated 'Insulin_signal' formula.
The user wants to fit the insulin binding and trafficking rates using the Schwen hepatocyte uptake data. The loss is defined as the mean squared difference between the log10 of the predicted and measured Insulin_signal for each record.
