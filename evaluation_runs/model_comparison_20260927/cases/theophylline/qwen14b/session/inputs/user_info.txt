Fit a one-compartment oral theophylline pharmacokinetic model to theophylline_sub6.csv.

Dataset columns:
- time in hours
- C_plasma, observed plasma concentration in mg/L.

Integrated states and initial values:
- A_gut(0) = 320.0 mg
- A_plasma(0) = 0.0 mg

Trainable parameters, all log scale:
- ka in [0.1, 10.0]
- ke in [0.01, 1.0]
- Vd in [5.0, 150.0]

ODE system:
- dA_gut/dt = -ka * A_gut
- dA_plasma/dt = ka * A_gut - ke * A_plasma

Derived observable:
- C_plasma = A_plasma / Vd

Loss:
- Compare simulated C_plasma to the measured C_plasma column.
- Normalize residuals by max(abs(measured C_plasma column)).
- loss = sqrt(mean(normalized squared residuals)).

Write out time, measured C_plasma, simulated C_plasma, simulated A_gut, and simulated A_plasma.
