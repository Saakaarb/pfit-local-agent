Fit the two-tank model to estimation.csv. The columns are time in seconds,
level_lower in volts and pump_voltage in volts. validation.csv is a separate
held-out record; do not fit it.

Let x1 be the upper tank level and x2 the lower tank level. At t=0, x1=5 and
x2=5.205. The model is
  dx1/dt = -k1 sqrt(max(x1,0)) + k4 u(t)
  dx2/dt = k2 sqrt(max(x1,0)) - k3 sqrt(max(x2,0))
Here u(t) is the measured pump_voltage, linearly interpolated between its
recorded times. Use the endpoint voltage outside the recorded interval.
Fit k1, k2, k3 and k4, each between 0.001 and 10 on a logarithmic scale.
Use this simple model without adding an overflow term.

The measured level_lower corresponds to x2. Minimize its RMSE divided by the
largest absolute measured level_lower value. The voltage is an input, so it
does not contribute a residual to the loss. Save time, measured lower level,
simulated lower and upper levels, and pump voltage.
