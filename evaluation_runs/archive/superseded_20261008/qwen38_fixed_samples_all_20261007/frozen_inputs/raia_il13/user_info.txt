Fit the IL-13 receptor/JAK2/STAT5 signalling model jointly to il13_4.csv and
il13_20.csv. These are the pSTAT5 time courses from the Raia lymphoma dataset.
Time is in minutes and the other column, pSTAT5, is the measured phosphorylated
STAT5 signal in the benchmark's units. The two experiments share all kinetic
parameters; their constant IL-13 concentrations are 4 and 20, respectively.

The states are Rec, Rec_i, IL13_Rec, p_IL13_Rec, p_IL13_Rec_i, JAK2, pJAK2,
STAT5, pSTAT5, SOCS3mRNA, DecoyR, IL13_DecoyR, SOCS3 and CD274mRNA.
At time zero Rec=1.3, Rec_i=235.058493601113, JAK2=2.8, STAT5=165,
and DecoyR=0.34. All the other biological states start at zero.
Keep these initial conditions fixed. The internal receptor initial value is
from the archived benchmark calibration rather than an independently measured
initial condition.

Let il13_level be constant within an experiment: 4 for il13_4.csv and 20 for
il13_20.csv. It can be carried as an extra state with zero derivative and the
corresponding initial value in each experiment.

Define the reaction rates as follows (products are indicated by spaces):
  v1 = 2.265 il13_level Kon_IL13Rec Rec
  v2 = Rec Rec_intern
  v3 = Rec_i Rec_recycle
  v4 = IL13_Rec Rec_phosphorylation pJAK2
  v5 = IL13_Rec JAK2 JAK2_phosphorylation / (1 + JAK2_p_inhibition SOCS3)
  v6 = JAK2 JAK2_phosphorylation p_IL13_Rec / (1 + JAK2_p_inhibition SOCS3)
  v7 = pRec_intern p_IL13_Rec
  v8 = pRec_degradation p_IL13_Rec_i
  v9 = 91 pJAK2 pJAK2_dephosphorylation
  v10 = STAT5 STAT5_phosphorylation pJAK2
  v11 = 91 pSTAT5 pSTAT5_dephosphorylation
  v12 = 2.265 il13_level DecoyR DecoyR_binding
  v13 = SOCS3mRNA_production pSTAT5
  v14 = SOCS3mRNA SOCS3_translation / (SOCS3mRNA + SOCS3_accumulation)
  v15 = SOCS3 SOCS3_degradation
  v16 = CD274mRNA_production pSTAT5

The balances are
  dRec/dt = -v1 - v2 + v3
  dRec_i/dt = v2 - v3
  dIL13_Rec/dt = v1 - v4
  dp_IL13_Rec/dt = v4 - v7
  dp_IL13_Rec_i/dt = v7 - v8
  dJAK2/dt = -v5 - v6 + v9
  dpJAK2/dt = v5 + v6 - v9
  dSTAT5/dt = -v10 + v11
  dpSTAT5/dt = v10 - v11
  dSOCS3mRNA/dt = v13
  dDecoyR/dt = -v12
  dIL13_DecoyR/dt = v12
  dSOCS3/dt = v14 - v15
  dCD274mRNA/dt = v16

Fit these shared parameters, each on a logarithmic scale from 0.00001 to 1000:
Kon_IL13Rec, Rec_intern, Rec_recycle, Rec_phosphorylation,
JAK2_phosphorylation, JAK2_p_inhibition, pRec_intern, pRec_degradation,
pJAK2_dephosphorylation, STAT5_phosphorylation, pSTAT5_dephosphorylation,
SOCS3mRNA_production, SOCS3_translation, SOCS3_accumulation, SOCS3_degradation.

Keep DecoyR_binding=0.003851039137402 and
CD274mRNA_production=0.021208853684209 fixed. Their branches do not feed back
into the pSTAT5 measurement in this model. The numerical factors 2.265 and 91
in the rate equations are also fixed.

For each experiment, calculate the RMSE in pSTAT5 and divide it by the largest
absolute measured pSTAT5 value in that experiment. Minimize the average of
these two normalized RMSEs, giving the experiments equal weight. Do not fit
any observation scaling or noise parameters. Use a stiff ODE solver, integrate
from time zero, and save measured/predicted pSTAT5 and all state trajectories
separately for the two experiments. These two time courses may leave some
rates weakly constrained; report that rather than assuming a unique estimate.
