Fit the Lotka-Volterra predator-prey model to lynx_hare.csv.

Dataset columns:
- time in years
- H, observed hare population in thousands
- L, observed lynx population in thousands

Integrated states and initial values:
- H(0) = 30.0
- L(0) = 4.0

Trainable parameters, all log scale:
- alpha in [0.1, 2.0]
- beta in [0.001, 0.5]
- delta in [0.001, 0.5]
- gamma in [0.1, 2.0]

ODE system:
- dH/dt = alpha * H - beta * H * L
- dL/dt = delta * H * L - gamma * L

Loss:
- Compare simulated H and L directly to the two measured columns.
- Use one normalized squared-error term per observed state.
- For each observed column, normalize residuals by max(abs(measured column)).
- loss = sqrt(mean(all normalized squared residuals)).

Write out time, measured H, measured L, simulated H, and simulated L.
